# Kilwer-Kultivator
🌱 Kilwer Kultivator — An iOS grow journal for tracking plants from seed to harvest, with detailed logs, photo timelines, environmental monitoring, and reminders.
🌱 Kilwer Kultivator

Grow smarter. Track everything. Cultivate better.

Kilwer Kultivator is an iOS cultivation journal designed to help growers organize, document, and track their plants from seed to harvest.

📱 Features

* Grow Management: Create and manage individual grows, including strains, stages, and start dates.
* Detailed Grow Logs: Record watering, feeding, environmental conditions, lighting, training, and more.
* Photo Timeline: Document plant development with dated photos.
* Grow Reminders: Stay organized with scheduled notifications.
* Environmental Tracking: Log temperature, humidity, VPD, and lighting information.
* Harvest & Curing Records: Keep track of the entire cultivation process.
